"# dreamy"
This is a dream diary with little extras.
With more extras

After writing the dream, we can specify the mood of the dream.
I wrote a dream dictionary consisting of nearly 100 keywords for the dream diary.
The diary can be viewed in JSON format.
For easier interpretation, a questioning horoscope is at our disposal. With South Indian illustrations.
This is a diagram that helps us meditate, so that we can find out what our dream is warning us about as soon as possible.
The yantra in the middle of the diagram helps our meditation even more.
